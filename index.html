Origins

Where Engineering Started

My journey into engineering started through mathematics and curiosity about structures, models and practical applicability.

While studying mathematics, I discovered computational modeling through Excel-based calculations and became interested in how systems process information and generate outcomes.

After being introduced to Python by a mathematics professor, I began studying programming independently through technical books, documentation and continuous experimentation.

Learning programming also unlocked my ability to read technical English documentation directly, gradually transforming both my communication and engineering learning process.

Over time, backend engineering evolved from curiosity into a continuous applied laboratory involving APIs, Flask ecosystems, databases, automation, operational workflows and systems architecture.
