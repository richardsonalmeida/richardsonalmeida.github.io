Engineering Approach

I approach software development by first understanding the problem and operating environment. Complexity is introduced only when justified by concrete needs, maintaining a closed-loop cycle of continuous verification.

01

Understand

Problem, context, and constraints. Analyze existing flows before choosing technology.

02

Design

Boundaries, architecture, and trade-offs. Make structure proportional to the problem.

03

Implement

Code, integration, and environments. Build reproducible and cleanly separated systems.

04

Validate

Execute, test, and observe. Verify behavior directly through operation.

05

Document

Decisions, evidence, and evolution. Record technical reasoning for future clarity.

06

Evolve

Learn, improve, and increase complexity only when operational evidence justifies it.

↺ Evidence Loop: The system informs the next cycle. Execution and real-world behavior continuously feed back into Understand.
